Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?

Orders ship within 1 business day. Delivery inside Italy takes 2–3 business days; the rest of the EU 4–7 business days.

Do you ship outside the EU?

Not yet. We plan to add the UK and Switzerland in 2027.

What is your return policy?

You can return any unused item within 30 days for a full refund. Return shipping costs € 4,90 unless the item was faulty.

How can I contact you?

Write to help@greennest.example or use the chat button — we answer within 24 hours on working days.
